Privacy Policy

Murti Devi Balika Vidyapeeth, Binauli, Baghpat · managed by M.D.D.G. Makhar Educational & Social Welfare Trust · Last updated 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information Murti Devi Balika Vidyapeeth ("the school", "we") keeps about students, parents and staff, why we keep it, and how it is protected. It covers our website mdbv-edu.in, the MDBV school portal, and the school's WhatsApp number +91 92865 90709.

Who is responsible

M.D.D.G. Makhar Educational & Social Welfare Trust, which runs Murti Devi Balika Vidyapeeth, Meerut-Baraut Road, Binauli, Baghpat, Uttar Pradesh 250345, India.
Contact: mdbvidyapeeth@gmail.com · Office phone +91 95572 60700.

What information we keep

  • Student records needed to run the school: name, class, date of birth, parents' names, address, admission details, attendance, marks, fees paid and due, and the records required by UDISE+ and the education department.
  • Parents' contact details: mobile and WhatsApp numbers, so the school can reach the family.
  • WhatsApp messages that a parent sends to the school number, and the school's replies, with their date and delivery status.
  • Staff records needed for employment, attendance and salary.
  • Portal logins: user name, role and the time of sign-in, to keep accounts secure.

How we use it

  • To teach, keep attendance and marks, and prepare report cards.
  • To send parents school messages on WhatsApp: fee reminders, absence alerts, results, parent-teacher meeting reminders and school notices.
  • To answer parents' questions sent to the school WhatsApp number. Some common questions (such as fees due, attendance or homework) are answered automatically from the school's records; before sharing any child's details, we ask the parent to confirm the child's date of birth or admission number.
  • To meet legal and government reporting duties.

We do not sell information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) carries the WhatsApp messages, under the WhatsApp Business terms.
  • Supabase stores the portal's data securely for the school.
  • Government education authorities (such as UDISE+ and the education department) receive the records the law requires.

Each of these only receives what is needed for that purpose.

How it is protected

Records are kept in a secured database. Only school staff with a portal login can see them, and each staff member only sees what their role needs.

How long we keep it

Student records are kept while the child studies at the school and afterwards for as long as the law or the education department requires (for example, admission registers and transfer certificates). WhatsApp messages are kept only as long as they are useful for school work, and parents can ask for them to be deleted at any time (see below).

Your choices, and deleting your information

  • Stop WhatsApp messages: send the school number a message saying you do not want messages, or tell the school office. We will stop non-essential messages to that number.
  • See or correct your information: ask the school office, or email us.
  • Delete your information: email mdbvidyapeeth@gmail.com or write to the school office with the parent's name, the WhatsApp number and the child's name. We will delete your WhatsApp messages and any information we are not legally required to keep within 30 days, and tell you when it is done.

Children

The school's WhatsApp number and portal messages are meant for parents and guardians. Students' information is handled by the school only for their education and safety.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
